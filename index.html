What Does the Gray X on Snapchat Mean and Why Does It Appear?

Seeing a gray X next to someone’s name on Snapchat can be confusing, especially if you were previously able to chat or send Snaps normally. The symbol may appear for different reasons depending on your friendship status, chat history, and Snapchat settings. Understanding what the symbol means can help you avoid assuming that something is wrong with your account.

The gray x on Snapchat generally appears next to a person when Snapchat does not currently recognize the connection in the same way as an active friendship. However, the exact situation can vary. It may be related to whether someone has added you back, whether a friend connection has changed, or whether Snapchat is displaying a pending interaction.

What Does the Gray X on Snapchat Mean?

The X on Snapchat is commonly associated with a connection that is not currently functioning like a normal mutual friendship. Snapchat uses different symbols and indicators to communicate the status of interactions between users.

For example, if you see an X beside a person's name instead of the usual camera or chat options, it may indicate that the person has not added you back or that the friendship connection has changed.

It is important not to assume that an X automatically means you have been blocked. Blocking, removing a friend, and having a pending friend request can produce different experiences within Snapchat.

Why Does Snapchat Show an X?

There are several possible reasons why the Snapchat x can appear.

1. The Person Has Not Added You Back

One common reason is that you have added someone, but they have not accepted or added you back. Snapchat may show an X because the connection is not currently mutual.

In this situation, you may still be able to see certain information depending on the person's privacy settings, but some communication features may be limited.

2. Someone Removed You

If you were previously friends with someone and suddenly notice an X, they may have removed you from their friends list.

When someone removes you, Snapchat can change the available interaction options. You might notice that the normal camera or chat options are no longer displayed in the same way.

However, the presence of an X alone does not provide enough information to determine exactly what happened.

3. A Friend Request Is Pending

A pending friend request can also cause Snapchat to display an X. This can happen when one person has sent a friend request but the other person has not accepted it.

The account may still appear in your contacts or recent conversations, making the situation confusing.

4. Snapchat's Interface Has Changed

Snapchat regularly updates its interface and features. Symbols can sometimes appear differently depending on the app version, device, account settings, or the location where you are viewing a conversation.

Therefore, if you suddenly notice a new symbol, checking whether your Snapchat app needs an update can be useful.

What Does the X on Snapchat Mean in Chat?

The meaning of the x on Snapchat can depend on where you see it.

If it appears next to a person's name in your Chat or Friends section, it may indicate that the person is not currently connected with you as a mutual friend. You may also see options that allow you to add the person again or manage the connection.

The best way to understand the situation is to look at the other information Snapchat provides. Check whether you can still view the person's profile, send a message, or see their Snap Score if it was previously visible.

These details can provide more context than the X symbol by itself.

What If There Is an X on Snapchat but You Are Still Friends?

One of the most confusing situations is seeing an x on Snapchat but still friends. If you believe you and the other person are still connected, there are several possibilities.

First, Snapchat may simply be displaying a temporary interface issue. Apps can occasionally experience synchronization problems, especially after an update or when account information has not refreshed properly.

You can try closing and reopening Snapchat to see whether the symbol disappears.

Another possibility is that Snapchat's friendship status has not updated correctly. Checking the person's profile can help you determine whether the friendship still appears normally.

If the X remains, updating Snapchat to the latest version and clearing temporary app data may also help.

Does a Gray X Mean Someone Blocked You?

Not necessarily.

A gray x on Snapchat should not automatically be interpreted as proof that someone blocked you. Snapchat provides limited visual information, and several different account situations can result in similar indicators.

If someone blocks you, their profile and interactions may become unavailable to you. However, an X by itself does not establish that you have been blocked.

It is better to consider the complete situation rather than relying on one symbol.

How to Check Your Snapchat Friendship Status

If you are trying to understand why the X is appearing, you can check the person's profile.

Look for information such as:

  • Whether you can still find their account.
  • Whether their profile shows that you are friends.
  • Whether you can send them a Chat or Snap.
  • Whether their Snap Score is visible.
  • Whether Snapchat gives you an option to add them.
  • Whether previous conversations are still available.

These indicators can help you understand whether the issue involves a pending request, removed friendship, privacy settings, or a temporary app problem.

Can You Remove the X on Snapchat?

There is no universal setting that simply removes the X. What you need to do depends on why the symbol is appearing.

If the person has not added you back, you may need to wait for them to accept your request. If you accidentally removed them, you can search for their username and send another friend request.

If you believe the X is appearing because of an app problem, try restarting Snapchat, checking for updates, and signing out and back in.

Avoid repeatedly sending requests if someone has intentionally removed you. Respecting another user's privacy and friendship preferences is important when using social media.

Final Thoughts

The Gray x on Snapchat can be confusing because it does not always point to one specific situation. It may indicate that a friendship is no longer mutual, that a request is pending, or that Snapchat is displaying a connection differently.

If you see an X on Snapchat, look at the person's profile and the communication options available to you before drawing conclusions. The Snapchat x alone does not necessarily mean that you have been blocked.

For situations involving an x on Snapchat but still friends, refreshing the app, checking your friendship status, and updating Snapchat can help determine whether the symbol is temporary or related to the account connection.

FAQs

What does the gray X on Snapchat mean?

The gray X can indicate that the connection between two Snapchat users is not currently a mutual friendship. It may appear when a friend request is pending or when someone has removed the other person.

Does the X on Snapchat mean I am blocked?

No. An X by itself does not confirm that someone has blocked you. Other account and profile information should be considered before determining what happened.

Why do I see an X on Snapchat but still have the person in my friends list?

This can happen because of an app synchronization issue, a pending account change, or Snapchat's interface. Try refreshing the app and checking the person's profile again.

Can the Snapchat X disappear?

Yes, depending on the reason it appeared. If a pending friend request is accepted or the friendship status changes, Snapchat may display different interaction options.

What is the difference between an X and other Snapchat symbols?

Snapchat uses different icons to represent friendship, messaging, Snaps, and other account interactions. Their meaning can depend on the specific screen and current version of the app.

Should I add someone again if I see a gray X?

If you believe you accidentally removed the person, you can send another friend request. However, if they intentionally removed you, it is better to respect their decision rather than repeatedly sending requests.
